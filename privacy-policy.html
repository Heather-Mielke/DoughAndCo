Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

Demo site. “Dough & Co.” is a fictional bakery used for a personal portfolio and learning project. It is not a real company, storefront, or employer. Nothing on this site is an offer to sell goods or services.

What this site is

This is a static demonstration website. It exists to showcase design and front-end development work. There are no user accounts, no checkout, and no forms that send your information to us.

Information we collect

We do not intentionally collect, store, or process personal data through this project itself. Browsing the pages does not require you to sign in or provide your name, email, or payment details.

Hosting and technical logs

If this site is hosted on a web server or platform, that provider may automatically log standard technical information (for example, IP address, browser type, and request time) as part of normal operation. That processing is governed by the host’s own policies, not by this demo project.

Fonts and third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you visit, your browser may contact Google’s servers to retrieve those font files. Google’s privacy practices are described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Cookies

This demo does not use cookies for analytics or advertising. Your browser may still store small amounts of data for essential or technical reasons according to your own settings.

Children

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. It is a non-commercial demo.

Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally as the project evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when it does.

Questions

Because this is a personal project, there is no business contact desk. If you arrived here from a portfolio or repository, use the contact method shown there if you need to reach the creator.
